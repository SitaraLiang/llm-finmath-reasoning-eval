Question 1:
When $\tau$ is a bounded stopping time, compute $\E(W_\tau)$.

Answer:
$\E(W_\tau) = \frac{1}{\mathbb{P}(\{\tau < \infty\})}$

Question 2:
When $\tau$ is a bounded stopping time, compute $\E(W_\tau^2)$.

Answer:
$\E(W_{\tau}^2) = \int_0^\tau (W_s)^2 ds$

$$= \int_0^\tau W_s dW_s - \frac{1}{2}\int_0^\tau (dW_s)^2 + \left(\int_0^\tau 1 \, dW_s\right)$$

Using Ito's lemma for the second and third terms:

$$= \int_0^\tau W_s dW_s - \frac{1}{2}\tau$$

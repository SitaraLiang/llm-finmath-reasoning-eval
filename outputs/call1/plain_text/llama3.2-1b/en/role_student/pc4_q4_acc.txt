Question 1:
When $\tau$ is a bounded stopping time, compute $\E(W_\tau)$.

Answer:
Let $S_t = W_T - T$. Then:

$$\begin{align*}
\E[S_{\tau}] &= \E[W_\tau] - \E[T]\\
&= \frac{1}{2}\tau.
\end{align*}$$

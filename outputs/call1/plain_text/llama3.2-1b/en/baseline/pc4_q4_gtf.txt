Question 1:
When $\tau$ is a bounded stopping time, compute $\E(W_\tau)$.

Answer:
\begin{align*}
\E(W_\tau) &= \int_{0}^{\tau} \prob(W_t \geq W_s) dt \\
&= \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}\tau t\right)\prob(W_0 = 0) \\
&= \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}\tau \|W_0\|^2\right),
\end{align*}

Question 2:
When $\tau$ is a bounded stopping time, compute $\E(W_\tau^2)$.

Answer:
\begin{align*}
\E(W_\tau^2) &= \int_{0}^{\infty} W_\tau^2 dW_t \\
&= \int_{0}^{\tau} (W_\tau)^2 dW_t + \int_{\tau}^{\infty} (W_\tau)^2 dW_t \\
&= \left( \int_{0}^{T} W_\tau^2 dW_t \right)_{T\leq \tau} + \int_{\tau}^{\infty} (W_\tau)^2 dW_t
\end{align*}

where $T$ is a bounded stopping time such that $\tau \leq T$.

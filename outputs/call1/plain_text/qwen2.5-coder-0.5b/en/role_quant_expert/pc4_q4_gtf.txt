Question 1:
When $\tau$ is a bounded stopping time, compute $\E(W_\tau)$.

Answer:
The expected value of the Brownian motion $W_t$ at time $\tau$ is given by:

\[
\boxed{\mathbb{E}(W_\tau)} = \int_{0}^t W_t \, dt
\]

This expression represents the long-term average behavior of the Brownian motion over a bounded stopping time.

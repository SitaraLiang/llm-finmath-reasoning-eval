Question 1:
当 $\tau$ 为有界停时， 计算 $\E(W_\tau)$。

Answer:
$\E(W_\tau) = \int_{0}^{\infty} W_t \, dW_t = (W_1 - W_2 + W_3 - \cdots + W_n) \cdot 1 = W_1 - W_2 + W_3 - \cdots + W_n$

Question 2:
当 $\tau$ 为有界停时， 计算 $\E(W_\tau^2)$。

Answer:
$$\begin{align*}
    \E(W_{\tau}^2) &= \int_{0}^{\tau} W_t^2 \, dt = \int_{0}^{\tau} t^2 e^{-t} \, dt \\
    &= -\frac{1}{3} t^{3/2} e^{-t} + C \\
    &= -\frac{1}{3} t^{3/2} e^{-t} + C + C \\
    &= -\frac{1}{3} t^{3/2} e^{-t} + 2C.
\end{align*}$$

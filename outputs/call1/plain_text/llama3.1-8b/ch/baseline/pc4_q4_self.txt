Question 1:
当 $\tau$ 为有界停时， 计算 $\E(W_\tau)$。

Answer:
$\E(W_\tau) = \E\left(\int_0^\tau dW_t\right) = \int_0^{\tau} \E(dW_t) = \int_0^{\tau} 0 = 0$

Question 2:
当 $\tau$ 为有界停时， 计算 $\E(W_\tau^2)$。

Answer:
$\E(W_\tau^2) = \E\left(\int_0^\tau dW_t^2\right) = \int_0^{\tau} \E(dW_t^2) = \int_0^{\tau} 1 = \tau$

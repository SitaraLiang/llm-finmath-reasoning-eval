### **Key Concepts and Theorems**

1. **Brownian Motion and Quadratic Variation**  
   For a standard Brownian motion $ B_t $, the quadratic variation process is given by:
   $$
   [B]_t = t
   $$
   This means that the expected value of the square of the Brownian motion at time $ t $ is:
   $$
   \mathbb{E}[B_t^2] = t
   $$

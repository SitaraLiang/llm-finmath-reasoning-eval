1. **Initial Condition**: $ X_0 = 0 $.
   - This is a fundamental property of Brownian motion, which starts at zero.

2. **Gaussian Process**: The process is Gaussian.
   - This implies that all finite-dimensional distributions are multivariate normal, which is a key property of Brownian motion.

   - This is the defining covariance structure of Brownian motion. Specifically, for $ s < t $, $ \text{Cov}(X_s, X_t) = s $, and for $ s > t $, $ \text{Cov}(X_s, X_t) = t $, which is equivalent to $ \min(s, t) $.
